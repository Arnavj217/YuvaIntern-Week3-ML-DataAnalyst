# Week 3 Public Source Research

## Primary dataset
Government of India OGD — District-wise, season-wise crop production statistics from 1997
https://data.gov.in/resource/district-wise-season-wise-crop-production-statistics-1997

The resource is published by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It is described as district-wise, crop-wise, season-wise and year-wise crop area and production data. The resource page reports annual granularity and an update on 16 October 2024.

## Supporting weather source
Government of India OGD — Rainfall in India
https://data.gov.in/catalog/rainfall-india

The catalogue is contributed by India Meteorological Department under the Ministry of Earth Sciences and contains monthly all-India rainfall plus sub-division rainfall and departure from normal for months and seasons.

## Market context
e-NAM
https://enam.gov.in/
e-NAM is a pan-India electronic trading portal connecting APMC mandis and provides agricultural market/price-discovery context.

## Data-integrity statement
The numerical records analyzed in this package are a synthetic sample modeled on the public crop-production schema. They are not official observations.
